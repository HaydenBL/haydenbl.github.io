Junteo Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Junteo does not collect any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no ads.

Your videos

Everything Junteo does happens on your device. Your videos are never uploaded to any server. They stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Photo library

Junteo uses your photo library to import the clips you pick and to save your finished video. If you choose to delete the original clips after an export, Junteo will ask for permission first. Your library is never accessed for any other reason.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Junteo never sees your payment information. For details, see the Apple Privacy Policy.
